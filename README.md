# Diploma Proof Editor

An interactive tool for Oxnard Union High School District staff to preview and give feedback on the Class of 2027 diploma design.

## What it does

- **Edit any text** directly on the diploma or in the side panel
- **Compare fonts** from the district's Adobe Fonts kit: Harbour Rough (last year's font), Amador, and LTC Goudy Text
- **Test student names**, including long, hyphenated, and accented names
- **Print-accurate sizes** in points at the final 8 × 6 in print size
- **Move and resize text boxes** with snap guides and a 0.5 in safe zone
- **Show or hide** the school crest and district seal
- **Export for feedback** as a 300 dpi JPG with an optional card listing the changes and comments
- **Reset to original** restores the district standard layout

## How to use

Open the live page, make your changes, then click **Export for feedback**. Download the JPG and send it to the district office along with your suggestions.

## Notes

- Fonts load from Adobe Fonts, so the page must be viewed from its web address, not opened as a local file.
- Edits are saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent automatically.
- The district's diploma standard remains the default. This tool is for gathering staff input.

Hosted with GitHub Pages. The page is a single self-contained file, `index.html`.
