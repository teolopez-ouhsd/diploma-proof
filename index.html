Font Size Tracking

The text doesn't fit in this box. Make the box bigger or the text smaller.

Drag the Move tab to reposition. Drag a corner or side handle to change the box size; the text wraps inside it and keeps its point size. Use the Size slider to change the text. If the text grows past the box, the box grows with it. With the Move tab focused, arrow keys nudge by 0.05 in (Shift for 0.25 in). X is the center of the line and Y is its top edge, both in inches.

Under 7 pt can be hard to read in blackletter once printed.

Sizes are in points at the printed size of 8 × 6 in, the same units you'd see in Illustrator. Tracking uses Illustrator's scale too.
